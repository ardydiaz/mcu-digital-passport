1. System Overview

The proposed MCU Digital Student Passport is a web-based, mobile-responsive platform designed to 
record, monitor, and validate student participation in university activities and the completion of assigned 
bucket list experiences. 
The system shall be accessible through the existing MCU Student Portal, subject to IT's assessment of 
integration feasibility. 

2. Functional Requirements 

    Student Account Integration
       - Automatically retrieve student information using existing MCU Student Portal credentials.
    
    Digital Passport 
       - Generate an individual digital passport for every enrolled student, containing their profile, unique QR code, and participation progress.

    Bucket List Module
       - Display the pre-identified bucket list experiences, including completion status and corresponding activity records.
    
    Activity Management
       - Allow authorized personnel to create, edit, schedule, and manage university activities eligible for passport crediting. 
    
    QR Code Attendance System
       - Enable authorized activity organizers to scan student QR codes to record attendance and participation. 
    
    Attendance Validation
       - Verify student participation before crediting an activity, with safeguards against duplicate entries.

    Automatic Crediting
       - Automatically update the student's digital passport upon successful validation of participation. 
    
    Digital Stamps 
       - Issue a digital stamp for every successfully completed and verified experience.
    
    Progress Monitoring 
       - Display the number of completed and remaining experiences, including overall completion percentage.

    Activity History
       - Maintain a chronological record of verified activities, including activity title, date, and completion status.
    
    Administrative Dashboard
       - Provide SASD with centralized access to participation records, student progress, and activity management.
    
    Report Generation
       - Generate downloadable reports in Excel or CSV format, filterable by student, college, program, activity, and date.
    
    Manual Validation
       - Allow authorized SASD personnel to manually credit, correct, or invalidate participation records with documented justification.

    Notifications 
       - Notify students when an experience has been successfully credited or when they achieve a completion milestone. 

3. User Access and Permissions 
     The system shall support role-based access for the following users: 
        1. Students: Access their digital passport, QR code, bucket list, progress, and activity history.
        2. Activity Organizers: Manage assigned activities, scan QR codes, and validate student attendance.
        3. SASD Administrators: Manage the passport system, approve or adjust participation records, and generate institutional reports.
        4. IT Administrators: Manage system configuration, integrations, access permissions, and technical maintenance.
